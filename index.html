01 / Review domains

A rigorous lens on medical AI outputs.

Generative AI can produce fluent medical text without reliably distinguishing sound recommendations from omissions or unsupported claims. Each review considers content within its intended audience and use case.

01
◎

Clinical accuracy

Check medical statements, diagnostic reasoning, treatment descriptions, and context-dependent claims for correctness.

02
⌁

Evidence integrity

Evaluate whether cited sources are real, relevant, current, and accurately represented; identify unsupported certainty.

03
⊕

Patient safety

Identify harmful advice, missing red flags, unsafe simplifications, and gaps that may alter clinical decisions.

04
≋

Completeness & nuance

Find consequential omissions, inappropriate generalization, and missing limitations or alternative explanations.

05
Aa

Communication quality

Review readability, terminology, tone, health literacy, and whether uncertainty is communicated responsibly.

06
↗

Actionable improvement

Translate findings into specific corrections and practical recommendations for safer, clearer content.

02 / Our approach

Consistent process.
Context-sensitive judgment.

Reviews are organized to make concerns traceable, explain the reasoning behind each finding, and distinguish factual errors from communication problems.

Outputs should support human review and quality improvement — not replace clinical care, regulatory assessment, or formal model validation.

  1. 01

    Define context

    Clarify the intended audience, content type, clinical setting, and limits of the review.

  2. 02

    Examine the content

    Assess claims and references against applicable clinical evidence and identify clinically important omissions.

  3. 03

    Document findings

    Record the concern, why it matters, suggested correction, and supporting rationale.

  4. 04

    Prioritize improvements

    Separate potentially safety-critical findings from substantial and editorial issues.

03 / Illustrative review

What a finding looks like.

A simplified, fictional example showing how reviewers can move from identifying a potentially misleading claim to proposing a safer formulation. This is not a real patient case.

AI-generated statement · Fictional example

“A normal test result always rules out the condition.”

Potential problem: absolute language without context.

Reviewer assessment

Potential safety concern

Overstatement of diagnostic certainty

Whether a normal result excludes a condition depends on test characteristics, timing, pre-test probability, and the clinical context.

Suggested improvement

“A normal result may reduce the likelihood of a condition, but it does not always exclude it. Interpretation should reflect the particular test and clinical circumstances.”

Illustrative educational content, not diagnostic advice.

04 / Guiding principles

Independent in perspective.
Transparent in method.

Responsible medical AI review calls for clearly stated assumptions, verifiable sources, proportionate safety concerns, and appropriate humility about what can be concluded from a single piece of content.

  • ✓ Evidence and provenance take priority over fluent wording.
  • ✓ Findings distinguish clinical risk from editorial quality.
  • ✓ Limitations, uncertainty, and conflicts of interest should be disclosed.
  • ✓ No identifiable patient information should be submitted through this website.
